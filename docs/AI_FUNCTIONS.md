# AI Functions

AHSAS utilizes AI to enhance student learning without bypassing critical thinking.

## 1. Socratic Seminar Synthesis (`netlify/functions/synthesize.js`)
- **Model:** Gemini AI via `GEMINI_API_KEY`.
- **Purpose:** Synthesizes seminar notes, generating summary reflections or follow-up Socratic questions based on student input.

## 2. Research Studio AI Assistance
- Provides guided feedback on historical research.
- Incorporates the Anti-AI C.E.C. Note Layer to ensure students originate their own Claims, Evidence, and Commentary rather than having the AI write it for them.
